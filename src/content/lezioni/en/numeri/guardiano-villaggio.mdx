## Quick review: addition with carrying

When you add in columns, start from the **ones**. If you get 10 or more, **ten ones become a ten**: write the ones and “carry” 1 into the tens column.

$$
\begin{array}{r}
 {}^{1}\,\,\, \\
 47 \\
 +\ 38 \\ \hline
 85
\end{array}
$$

7 + 8 = 15: write **5**, carry **1**. Then 4 + 3 + 1 = **8**. Result: **85**.

<Attenzione distrattore="barone">
The Baron of Panic blows the problem up: “Three-digit numbers? Impossible!” But a three-digit sum is just **three small sums one after another**:
ones, tens, hundreds. One step at a time, the dragon shrinks back into a lizard.
</Attenzione>

<Esempio titolo="Worked example: shopping at the market">
You buy bread for **136** cents and cheese for **275** cents.
Ones: 6 + 5 = 11, write 1 and carry 1. Tens: 3 + 7 + 1 = 11, write 1 and carry 1. Hundreds: 1 + 2 + 1 = 4.
Total: **411** cents, that is 4 euros and 11 cents.
</Esempio>
