## Comparing two fractions

To find out which fraction is bigger, bring them to the **same denominator** and compare the numerators:

$$
\frac{3}{4} = \frac{6}{8} \quad\text{and}\quad \frac{6}{8} > \frac{5}{8} \quad\Rightarrow\quad \frac{3}{4} > \frac{5}{8}
$$

Or use cross-multiplication: $3 \times 8 = 24$ against $4 \times 5 = 20$. The side with the bigger product wins.

<Esempio titolo="Worked example: the better discount">
Shop A: **1/4** off the price. Shop B: **3/10** off. Which is the better deal?
Common denominator 20: $\tfrac{1}{4} = \tfrac{5}{20}$ and $\tfrac{3}{10} = \tfrac{6}{20}$. Shop B’s discount is bigger.
</Esempio>
